## Exercises

Open exercises from the lesson, to try on your own. They have no answer files: work them out, and use the labs above as reference.

### Run the first ten minutes

1. Take one product you use daily. Write its four design questions as questions.
2. Answer only the first two, with numbers you can defend out loud.
3. Write one decision record: context, decision, rejected, consequence.
4. Now say which requirement would change your decision if it doubled.

> **Hint:** If a number is hard to guess, guess an order of magnitude and label it a guess. A labelled guess is usable; an unlabelled one is a trap.

## Check yourself

- What are the four questions a design has to answer, and which three decide its shape?
- Why is naming a database in the first minute of a discussion a mistake?
- In a forty five minute budget, why does the deep dive get more time than the high level design?
- What are the four lines of a decision record, and which one is most often missing?
- What is the observable symptom, during an outage, of a design that recorded no decisions?
